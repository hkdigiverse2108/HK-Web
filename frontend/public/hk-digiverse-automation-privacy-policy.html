1. Who this policy covers

We use our automation on Instagram professional account(s) belonging to HK DigiVerse team members who have authorised us to do so (the "connected account"). Everything the automation does happens through the connected account: it reads comments on the connected account's own posts and messages sent to it, and it replies, sends direct messages, or hides/deletes comments from that same account.

We do not use any other person's or business's Instagram or Facebook account to send replies or messages. If we later offer this service to other businesses, each business will connect and authorise its own account, and we will update this policy before we do.

This policy applies to people who comment on posts of the connected account, people who send messages to it, and the connected account holder.

2. Information we process

We process only what Meta's APIs return for the permissions granted, and only what the automation needs.

From people who comment on a post of the connected account

  • Comment text and comment ID
  • Public username or profile identifier
  • Identifier of the post or reel the comment was made on
  • Date and time of the comment

From people who message the connected account

  • Message text
  • The sender's app-scoped identifier assigned by Meta, and the identifier of the connected account
  • Message and conversation identifiers, and date and time

From the connected account holder

  • Instagram account identifier and username
  • Access tokens issued by Meta that let our automation act on the account
  • Automation settings, such as keywords, reply templates, and moderation rules

We do not ask for your Meta password and we do not access private profile information beyond what Meta's APIs provide.

3. How we use information

  • Detect new comments and messages on the connected account
  • Post automated public replies to comments
  • Send automated direct messages and private replies, where Meta's platform rules allow
  • Hide or delete comments according to rules set by the connected account holder
  • Use an AI provider to classify comments and messages or draft replies, where a workflow needs it
  • Log and troubleshoot interactions so the service works reliably
  • Protect the security of our systems and prevent abuse
  • Comply with legal obligations and Meta's platform requirements

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not use data received from Meta for advertising, profiling, or building user profiles.
  • We do not use data received from Meta to train artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.
  • We do not use or share the data for any purpose other than the automation described in this policy.
  • We do not share data with data brokers or unrelated third parties.

5. Processing through n8n

We use n8n, a workflow automation tool, to receive events from Meta (through webhooks and Instagram integrations), apply our rules, and perform actions such as replying to a comment or sending a message. n8n is self-hosted by us on a virtual private server (VPS) with PostgreSQL as its database and Redis for queueing and temporary state. We do not use n8n's hosted cloud service.

n8n execution records may temporarily include comment or message text and identifiers. They are deleted automatically on the schedule in Section 7.

8. Data deletion requests

You can ask us to delete personal information that we process through our Instagram or Facebook integrations. You do not need an account with us to make a request.

How to request deletion

  1. Email hkdigiverse@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request".
  2. Include your Instagram username or Facebook profile name and, if you can, the link or ID of the comment or conversation.
  3. We may ask you to confirm the request comes from you, for example by messaging us from the same account.

What happens next

  • We acknowledge your request within 3 business days.
  • We delete or anonymize the matching data from our database and workflow logs within 30 days and confirm by email.
  • Data held by Meta is outside our control. Contact Meta directly for that data.

9. Your rights

You may ask us for access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us using the details in Section 15. Where the law of India, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, or another applicable law gives you additional rights, we will honour them.

10. Security

We use measures such as HTTPS/TLS for data in transit, verification of the signature on webhook requests received from Meta, restricted access to our servers and n8n, storage of access tokens as protected credentials, and automatic deletion of old logs. No internet-based service can be guaranteed completely secure. If we become aware of a breach affecting your data, we will take steps to contain it and notify affected people and authorities where the law requires.

11. Where data is processed

Our servers are located in India (Mumbai). Meta and our AI providers may process data in other countries, including the United States.

12. Meta Platforms

We access Instagram and Facebook through APIs provided by Meta Platforms, Inc. and its affiliates, and we comply with Meta's Platform Terms and Developer Policies. Information that Meta itself processes is governed by Meta's own terms and privacy policies. We are not operated by or affiliated with Meta.

13. Children

Our services are not directed at children under 13 (or the higher minimum age set by law in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child's information has been provided to us, contact us and we will delete it.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services, integrations, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.
